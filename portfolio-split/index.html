Growing up in Stouffville, Ontario, and watching my father follow through on promises even when he was busy shaped values I still hold today — reliability and discipline in how I collaborate and deliver on commitments. A HIGH5 strengths assessment later identified me as a Time Keeper, Empathizer, Deliverer, Strategist, and Winner — labels that felt accurate the moment I read them.

These five strengths complement each other in a software context: the Time Keeper keeps projects on schedule, the Strategist makes sure we're solving the right problem, the Deliverer keeps commitments to teammates, the Empathizer keeps the end user present in every design decision, and the Winner stays hungry even on routine tasks.
